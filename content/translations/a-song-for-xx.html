I didn’t wish for a single one of those words

How long will I wait

The sun is already setting, It’s about time for me to go

[___] can't stay in the same place forever.

“Putting faith in people” – [though certainly] that faith will someday be betrayed
I believed it was the same as being flat rejected by them.

At that time, I couldn’t find such a strength anywhere

Certainly, I knew too much about a lot of things.

笑うことさえ苦痛になってた

Itsumo tsuyoi ko da nette iwaretsuzuketeta

Nakanai de erai nette homeraretari shiteita yo

Sonna fuu ni mawari wa ieba iuhodo ni

Warau koto sae kutsuu ni natteta

They always were continuously saying “you’re a strong kid”

“You’re so good for not crying” they praised me

And like that, the more the people around me said it

Even laughing became painful

一人きりで生まれて 一人きりで生きて行く

きっとそんな每日が当たり前と思ってた

Hitori kiri de umarete hitori kiri de ikite yuku

Kitto sonna hibi ga atarimae to omotteta

I was born alone, so I will live on alone

Certainly, I thought such a way of living like that

was only obvious.

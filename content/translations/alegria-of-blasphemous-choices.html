“The Alegria of Blasphemous Choices”

Allegro is Italian for 'lively' or 'cheerful' and it's a term used on musical scores to indicate that the piece should be played at a relatively fast pace and in a bright and merry manner. It's a similar term to vivace, which also means 'lively'.

I do not know if using the female version changes much other than sort of separating itself from the (commonly musically used) Allegro but functionally I would say that it means “bliss” or “cheerfulness” in the context of the song.

Also, it can be “Choice” or “Choices” – there is no way to tell as it doesn’t matter

Observations during / after translations:

The singer, Mayumi Morinaga usually goes by the doujin handle “Senya” but in this case she is using her own name for this – Frost Fragment is a sort of offshoot of Yuuhei Satellite (the difference between the two is paper thin, they probably made a new ‘circle’ to ‘return to their roots’ because this song is more like their old work than their new stuff.

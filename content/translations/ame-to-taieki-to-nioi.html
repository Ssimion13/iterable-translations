雨と体液と匂い

“Rain, Bodily Fluids, and Scent”

[イントロ]

濡れた指は　艶やかに誘う

慰めてと呟いた

しなる背中　締め付けた夜

[Intro]

Nureta yubi wa tsuyayaka ni sosou

Nagusamete to tsubuyaita

Shinaru senaka shizumetsuketa yoru

Wetted fingers lustrously inviting me

And when they comfort me, they murmur

A bent back in a suffocating night

[バース1]

操られた　僕の理性など

捻じ伏せた官能　に追う君の肌

[Verse 1]

Ayatsurareta boku no risei nado

Nejifuseta kannou ni ou kimi no hada

Something like my manipulated sense of reason

A twisted sensuality chasing after your skin

[バース2]

溶ける様に　求め繋いで

囁いた君の声に　従い続けて

熱を帯びた頬を　赤く染めて

「離れたくないの」と

　[Verse 2]

Tokeru you ni motome tsunaide

Sasayaita kimi no koe ni shitagai tsuzukete

Netsu wo obita hoho(?) wo akaku somete

[hanaretakunai no] to”

Searching for connection as if melting away

Your whispering voice continues to control me

My feverish cheeks are stained red

As if saying “I don’t want to let you go”

[コーラス]

白い布に　染みついた汚れ

僕らもう引き返す

事が出来ず　深い闇が誘う

[Chorus]

Shiroi nino(?) ni sumitsuita kegare

Bokura mou hikikaesu

Koto ga dekizu fukai yami ga sasou

An impurity, stained on a white sheet

We’re already unable to turn back

Invited by the deep darkness

[バース3]

指先触れ　見つけたのは君

僕の口を塞いだ　君の唇は　冷えていて

震えながらに　抱きしめて

僕の中に　溶け込んで一つに

[Verse 3]

Yubisaki fure mitsuketa no wa kimi

Boku no kuchi wo fusai da kimi no kuchibiru wa hieteite

Yurue nagara ni dakishimete

Boku no naka ni tokekonde hitotsu ni

My fingertips feel out, and what they found was you

My mouth is plugged up - your lips, cold to the touch

Embracing each other while shivering

Melting into one inside of me

[バース4]

濡れた肌を撫でて　吐息漏らし

虚ろな夢の様に

[Verse 4]

Nureta hada wo nadete toiki nurashi

Utsuro na yume no you ni

Stroking your wetted hands and leaking a sigh

As if in a hollow dream

[ブリッジ]

指を噛む　舌が触れた

微かに甘く　香り立つ

[Bridge]

Yubi wo kamu shita ga fureta

Kasuka ni amaku kaori tatsu

Biting your fingers as my tongue felt them

As a dimly sweet fragrance takes form

[コーラス]

匂い刺さる　部屋は

薄暗く雨の音と響く声

胸の鼓動

痛む程早く　鳴り響く

[Chorus]

Nioi sasaru heya wa

usugukuraku ame no oto to hibiku koe

Mune no kodou

Itamu hodo hayaku narihibiku

The room, pierced by the smell,

With the gloomy sound of rain and a resounding voice

The beating of my heart , so fast that it hurts

begins to echo

流れてく　時の中ででも　気だるさが　ほらグルグル廻って

私から　離れる心も　見えないわ　そう知らない？

Nagareteku toki no naka de demo kedarusa ga hora guruguru mawatte

Watashi kara hanareru kokoro mo mienai wa sou shiranai

Even stuck in flowing time, (this) listlessness revolves round and round
I can’t even see my own heart separating from me, I just don’t know

自分から　動くこともなく　時の隙間に　流され続けて

知らないわ　周りのことなど　私は私　それだけ

Jibun kara ugoku koto mo naku toki no sukima ni nagasare tsuzukete

Shiranai wa mawari no koto nado watashi wa watashi sore dake

Unable to move of my own accord, continually draining into a crevice in time

I don’t even understand what’s going on around me, I am me, and that’s it.

夢見てる？　なにも見てない？　語るも無駄な　自分の言葉

悲しむなんて　疲れるだけよ　何も感じず　過ごせばいいの

Yume miteru? Nani mo mitenai? Kataru mo muda na jibun no kotoba

Kanashimu nante tsukareru dake yo nani mo kanjizu sugoseba ii no

Am I seeing a dream? Am I seeing nothing? My words are meaningless even if I speak them

I’m not feeling sad – I’m just tired, it’d be better if I felt nothing at all.

戸惑う言葉　与えられても　自分の心　ただ上の空

もし私から　動くのならば　すべて変えるのなら　黒にする

Tomadou kotoba ataerarete mo jibun no kokoro tada uwa no sora

Moshi watashi kara ugoku no naraba subete kaeru no nara kuro ni suru

Even if you told me something truly perplexing, my heart would never notice (lit. above the sky)

If I could move (away) from myself, and that made everything change, (then) it’d only get worse (turn ‘black’)

こんな自分に　未来はあるの？　こんな世界に　私はいるの？

今切ないの？　今悲しいの？　自分の事も　わからないまま

Konna jibun ni mirai wa aru no? Konna sekai ni watashi wa iru no?

Ima setsunai no? Ima kanashii no? Jibun no koto mo wakaranai mama

Does someone like me have a future?
Can I live in a world like this?
Am I miserable right now?
Am I sad right now?
(I’m in such a state that) I don’t even know myself.

歩むことさえ　疲れるだけよ　人のことなど　知りもしないわ

こんな私も　変われるのなら　もし変われるのなら　白になる

Ayumu koto sae tsukareru dake yo hito no koto nado shiri mo shinai wa

Konna watashi mo kawareru no nara moshi kawareru no nara shiro ni naru?

Even just walking leaves me exhausted; I don’t even understand other people
If someone like me could change, if I changed, would I be okay (lit. turn ‘white’)?

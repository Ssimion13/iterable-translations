Doubts and Shadows overflowed (in me), but no matter how many times that happens, I rid* myself of them and keep walking forward in this world

My awoken heart dashes to draw the future

In a difficult road that I’m stuck at

The sky is blue and beautiful and always is waiting to come

So, because of that, I’ll never be afraid

So, with that said, no matter what comes, I won’t lose heart.

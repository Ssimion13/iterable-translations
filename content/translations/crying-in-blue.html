かわいた都会が　モノクロに

硝子 (ガラス)の夢をくだいてしまう

遠い記憶のやさしい想い

もう手のひらをこぼれ

Kawaita tokai ga monochro(me) ni

(Glass) no yume no kudaite shimau

Tooi kioku no yasashii omoi

Mou tenohira wo kobore

The desiccated city turns monochrome

The dream made of glass is shattered

The tender thoughts of memories long past

Already slip away from my hands

ひとりきりで瞳(め)をとじて

心のありかを捜すけど

迷いかけた溜息が

ブルーに泣いてる

Hitori kiri de me wo tojite

Kokoro no arika wo sagasu kedo

Mayoikaketa tameiki ga

Blue ni naiteru

All on my own, I close my eyes

Even though I look for the whereabouts of my heart

A sigh of one who has lost their way

Cries in blue

ことばの向こうでちぐはぐに

誰かが罠をはりめぐらして

抱いているほど抱かれてるようなら

なぜ振り切れもしない

Kotoba no mukou de chikuhagu ni

Dareka ga wana wo harimegurashite

Daite iru hodo dakareteru you nara

Naze furikire mo shinai

Behind every word is a messily laid

Trap laid out by someone

If the more you embrace,

more you seem to be embraced…

Then why can’t I let go?

ひとりきりで見てるのは

鏡の疲れた背中だけ

まぎれこんだ夜までが

ブルーに泣いてる

Hitori kiri de miteru no wa

Kagami no tsukareta senaka dake

Magirekonda yoru made ga

Blue ni naiteru

All on my own, what I am seeing

Is my tired back in the mirror

Until I slip away into the night

crying in blue

ひとりきりで行けるのに

心は誰かを待っている

ふりかえれば風さえも

ブルーに泣いてる

Hitori kiri de ikeru no ni

Kokoro wa dareka wo matteiru

Furikaereba kaze sae mo

Blue ni naiteru

Even though I can keep going all on my own,

My heart waits for someone

If I could look back, even the wind

Would be crying in blue

生まれくるのは星の生命で 夜空 埋めてく色とり どりの想こえ 儚き記億　伝う涙に 誰かの願い　そっと触れて消えた

In the birth of a star, the night sky is filled with myriad voices..In the tears of transient memories, I softly feel someone’s wish, only for it to vanish..

 umarekuru no wa hoshi no inochi de yozora umeteku irotoridori no koe hakanaki kioku tsutau namida ni dareka no negai sotto furete kieta

輝いてキエルこえと星の降る夜に何を思う？ 過去の想こえ　耳を澄まし風が吹く場所で眺めていた

The shining, disappearing voices in the night with stars falling from the sky...what do they think?

My ears hear the voices of the past.

The wind blows at the spot you were looked at.

 kagayaite kieru koe to hoshi no furu yoru ni nani wo omou? kako no koe mimi wo sumashi kaze ga fuku basho de nagameteita

生まれて巡る生命の日々 時を紡いで積み重ねた歴史 拙い言葉　君を求めて 私の願い受け継がれてゆくの

Birth is an everyday occurence.

Time is spun and built up in history.

I cannot describe in words my search --my wish is for you to inherit my wish.

Flowing in a flock of people, this, I devote myself to this current, changing world.

Even now, there are feelings in you that one day, in a dream, will suddenly break you into tears.

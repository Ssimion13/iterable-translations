Endless Waltz

Dandelion Trio

(Voice 1)

あの懐かしい香りが

暗い暗い底からのもの

咲いた蕾

わたしを

甘く甘く誘う

Ano natsukashii kaori ga

Kurai kurai soko kara no mono

Saita tsubomi

Watashi wo

Amaku amaku sasou

That nostalgic aroma is something

Of the darkest darkest depths

I, A blossomed flower bud,

am sweetly seduced by it.

(Voice 2)

こころの中の考えは

もう誰も知りたくない

怖い怖い「もの」には

もう触りたくない

Kokoro no naka no kangae wa

Mou daremo shiritakunai

Kowai kowai [mono] ni wa

Mou sawaritakunai

The thoughts inside one’s heart,

I no longer want to know them

Scary, scary things –
I don’t want to feel them anymore…

(Both)

踊って　緩やかなワルツ

華やかな舞台で踊りましょう

今は夢を翳したら

この心臓は鳴りやまないかもしれない

Odotte yuruyaka na WARUTSU

Hanayaka na butoi de odorimashou

Ima wa yume wo kazushitara

Kono shindou wa nari yama nai kamoshirenai

Dance, in a loose and gentle Waltz!

Let us dance in a beautiful stage performance

If you were to now hold your dreams aloft,

Then this throbbing heartbeat may go on forever…

踊って　緩やかなワルツ

愛しい自分と踊りましょう

夢に夢の夢色を見た

痛みは今も疼いているかな

Odotte yuruyaka na WARUSTU

Itoshii jibun to odorimashou

Yume ni yume no yumeiro wo mita

Iyami wa ima mo uzukuite iru kana

Dance, in a loose and gentle Waltz!

Let’s dance with our beloved self.

To have saw in a dream

the color of dreams within a dream –

That pain, does it still throb even now I wonder?

The two characters singing are both Satori –

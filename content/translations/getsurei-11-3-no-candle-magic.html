Like most Touhou songs, The singer sings from the perspective of a character: in this case it is of the character Patchouli Knowledge, a sickly magician who lives in the Scarlet Devil Mansion, a western styled Japanese estate. She is always reading and thus is very knowledgeable and constantly searching for new information. Whether she is willingly or unwillingly stuck in the library varies by interpretation – The mistress of the estate, Remilia Scarlet (usu. Referred to as a princess) can manipulate the concept of fate, which means that by living with Remilia, (on paper, anyways) Patchouli’s [physical, real] fate is entirely in the hands of Remilia. That makes up most of the background drama of this song.

“I love you!”

This song is about being trolled by God.

Patchouli is fated to never be more than what she currently is, and so she escapes into the dreamy world of books….only to find that even there she finds the same stories with the same princesses she wants to avoid, and even worse, all the stories end the same (and nothing in either fiction nor reality can be changed), and so, in the last line, she accepts it and says “I love you” to fate itself. That’s how I read it, anyway.

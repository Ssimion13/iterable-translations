癒えぬ痕

“Unhealing Scars”

-Original Song: 3rd Eye~ Satori Maiden

心の奥に潜む毒　人は笑顔の裏に隠し持つ

絶望、裏切り恐れて　真実追い求める･･･

A lurking poison lies In the depths of the heart -- hidden in the smiles of people lie despair and hopelessness, these same people, afraid of betrayal, pursue the truth...

紡がれる優しい嘘は　人を守る思いやりの言葉

傷つく事を知りながら　探る言葉の裏

Spinning tender lies, people protect themselves with words of sympathy…

Despite knowing their wounds, they feel about for the words they haven’t heard...

貴方は嘘に守られて　幸せに生きることができるの。

なのに真実を求めて　あるその幸せ気付くことはなく･･･

But you know, by protecting yourself with lies, you can live in happiness...

Even so, you search for truth, unable to notice your happiness...

聞こえ求める言葉は刃　人を傷付け悲しませるモノ

そんな心を忍ばせる　人の悲しい性よ

The words you seek are like blades that people hurt themselves and regret with

Concealing such a reality...people have sorrow written into their very nature...

私だけに聞こえてくる　静かに日々をただ送りたいの

ささやかなこの想いさえ　打ち砕かれていく･･･

Only I can hear these words; every day everyone wants to quietly live an ordinary daily life...

But even these modest feelings are so easily shattered...

貴方の禍つその心　紡がずにいれば聞こえはしない

一つ希望の貴方さえ　私から離れ消えてゆく運命

This unfortunate heart of yours, if I told you the truth you wouldn’t even hear it… The one hope of yours is a fate destined to disappear when I leave you...

可愛い子は語ることはない　私に抱かれて甘えて跳ねて

眠る時さえ側にいて　― 涙の痕もこれで癒えてゆくの？ ―

Cute children don’t speak of it, you jumping into my arms and bouncing…

Even at bed time, I am close to you; will you recover from this after you cry?

Well, this took a hella long time to translate. A LOT of the lines are hard to translate.

Kafuka Naru Gunjou He (Into Kafka’s Ultramarine)

Yuuhei Satellite

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d2mGV7NOlEQ

-------------------------------------------------------

生まれたことにすべて 人が求める都合良い理由なんて あるのだろうか？ Umareta koto ni subete Hito ga motomeru tsugou ii riyuu nante aru no darou ka?

(I wonder if) People want to know if there is a good reason why/how they were born/created?

------

望まれる価値ばかり考えすぎて 望むことのジレンマ　胸が痛いよ Nozomareru kachi bakari kangae sugite Nozomu koto no jirenma mune ga itai yo

Thinking too hard of the values that I should have…the dilemma of thinking about these things makes my heart hurt.

--------

だからこそ今変わらなければ 意中の儚さに 負けず　目を逸らさずにゆけ Dakara koso ima kawaranakereba Ichuu no hakanasa ni Makezu me wo sora sazu ni yuke

For this reason, we must be able to change.

Don't lose to the fickleness of your heart, keep looking forward *literally: don’t look away*, and go!

Main Chorus Line:

理性を強いられるから 君との駆け引きが出来る 望むことさえも 今正義に思う 僕らは生まれた日から 唯一無二の思想家さ 怖けずに進め カフカなる群青へ Risei wo shiirareru kara Kimi to no kake hiki ga dekiru Nozomu koto sae mo Ima wa seigi ni omou Bokura wa imareta hi kara Yuitsumuni no shisouka sa Ojikezu ni susume Kafuka naru gunjou he

Your reason compels me to negotiate with you, wishing only that what I believe now is just. From the day we were born we were able to think for ourselves, so don't be afraid and attain** Kafka's Ultramarine!*

*Kafka, as in, that German Philosopher guy. You could translate “Ultramarine” as a figurative way of saying “His world” or “His viewpoints”, but really, it’s most likely one of those “Hey, lets use Katakana and make it sound cool.” It literally means Ultramarine. There is no other translation for that Kanji.

**This could be like, a thousand different things. To become, to be, to do, to become like, to attain, to metamorphose, to go…to…let’s just go with “attain.”

------

生まれたことにすべて 僕が求める都合良い理由なんて 無いと悟った Imareta koto ni subete Boku ga motomeru tsugou ii riyuu nante Nai to satotta

I am aware that there is no convenient and good reason to explain why I was born.

君が持つ掴めない魅力に 負けたくない気持ち そして強くなると決めた Kimi ga motu tsukame nai miryoku ni Makenaku nai kimochi Soshite tsuyoku naru to kimeta

You don't possess the appeal to hold onto the feelings that you don't want to lose. Because of this, you should become determined to become stronger.

(Repeat Chorus Line)

享楽に染まることさえ 安らぎの頂点だろう 至福と堕落は 紙一重なんだろう 僕らは生まれた日から 唯一無二の思想家さ 怖けずに進め カフカなる群青へ * Kyouraku ni somaru koto sae Yasuragi no chouten darou Shifuku to daraku wa Kamihitoe nandarou Bokura wa umareta hi kara Yuitsumuni no shisouka sa Ojikezu ni susume Kafuka naru gunjou he

Even the smallest tainted pleasure can give you the apex of happiness.

However, there is a fine line between the bliss of having a peace of mind (eg. reaching zen) and becoming corrupted.

From the day we are born, we can think for ourselves, so don't fear and attain Kafka's Ultramarine (?)

(Repeat Chorus Line…again)

生まれた理由なんて 僕が望んだ掛け替えのない意思で 進めばいいんだ Umareta riyuu nante Boku ga nozonda kakegae no nai ishi de Susumeba iinda

I don’t desire a reason for why I was born. I’ll just go on with the irreplaceable intention I have, I guess…

生まれたことにすべて 人が求める都合良い理由なんて あるのだろうか？ Umareta koto ni subete Hito ga motomeru tsugou ii riyuu nante aru no darou ka?

All people search for such a thing as a “good reason” for why they were born, aren’t they?

------

望まれる価値ばかり考えすぎて 望むことのジレンマ　胸が痛いよ Nozomareru kachi bakari kangae sugite Nozomu koto no jirenma mune ga itai yo

Thinking only of the “values” others expect from them…

The ‘dilemma’ of those expectations hurts them to the core.

--------

だからこそ今変わらなければ 意中の儚さに 負けず目を逸らさずにゆけ Dakara koso ima kawaranakereba Ichuu no hakanasa ni Makezu me wo sora sazu ni yuke

That's why you must change now, without losing to the fragility of your intentions, and without looking away.

Main Chorus Line:

理性を強いられるから 君との駆け引きが 出来る 望むことさえも 今は正義に思う 僕らは生まれた日から 唯一無二の思想家さ 怖けずに進め カフカなる群青へ Risei wo shiirareru kara Kimi to no kake hiki ga dekiru Nozomu koto sae mo Ima wa seigi ni omou Bokura wa umareta hi kara Yuitsumuni no shisouka sa Ojikezu ni susume Kafuka naru gunjou he

Because we are bound by reason, I can negotiate with you;

The only thing I’m wishing for right now is to believe in what I think is right.

We are all unique thinkers from the day we are born, (aren’t we?)

Moving forward, without fear, to the kafka-esque blue!

(The word I translate as negotiate, kakehiki is like when you’re ‘bartering’ with someone, but in this (and its usual) context it means more like ‘talking with someone for the purpose of getting a good deal with them)

*Kafka, as in, the philosopher. Perhaps its a reference to the Blue Octavo notebooks about the human condition, since Gunjou=ultramarine which roughly is blue, but that is unlikely. Its more likely that gunjou is a metaphor of some sort to a state of calmness/being? I don’t know.

------

生まれたことにすべて 僕が求める都合良い理由なんて 無いと悟った umareta koto ni subete Boku ga motomeru tsugou ii riyuu nante Nai to satotta

I have searched everything for a reason for why I was born…

But I’ve come to realize that such a convenient thing as that doesn’t exist.

君が持つ掴めない魅力に 負けたくない気持ち そして強くなると決めた Kimi ga motsu tsukame nai miryoku ni Makenaku nai kimochi Soshite tsuyoku naru to kimeta

I feel that I don’t want to lose to the incomprehensible charm you have, so I made up my mind to become stronger.

(Repeat Chorus Line)

享楽に染まることさえ 安らぎの頂点だろう 至福と堕落は 紙一重なんだろう 僕らは生まれた日から 唯一無二の思想家さ 怖けずに進め カフカなる群青へ * Kyouraku ni somaru koto sae Yasuragi no chouten darou Shifuku to daraku wa Kamihitoe nandarou Bokura wa umareta hi kara Yuitsumuni no shisouka sa Ojikezu ni susume Kafuka naru gunjou he

Being soaked in pleasure is the apex of serenity, isn’t it?

But the line between true bliss and depravity is paper thin, right?

We are all unique thinkers from the day we are born (aren’t we?),

move forward, without fear, to the kafkaesque blue!

(Repeat Chorus Line…again)

生まれた理由なんて 僕が望んだ 掛け替えのない意思で 進めばいいんだ Umareta riyuu nante Boku ga nozonda kakegae no nai ishi de Susumeba iinda

I wished for a reason for why I was born, (but I think it’s)

better if I move forward with my irreplaceable will..

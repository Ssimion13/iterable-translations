鬼哭慟哭地獄楽

体捨て　なお残る恨みの魂がひしめく 此処は地獄

Here, where the souls filled with resentment who have shed their bodies wander about--

This is (the place known as) hell.

 karada sute nao nokoru urami no tamashii ga hishimeku koko wa jigoku

暗き地底　照らすは舞い遊ぶ怨嗟の灯 赤く深く

The dark underground depths are lit in a deep red by the light of deeply held, dancing resentment.

 kuraki chitei terasu wa maiasobu ensa no tomoshibi akaku fukaku

そう此処こそ世の果て　恨みを赦す地 ようこそ灼熱地獄 止まらぬ鬼哭止めたいなら さあついて来て

So, here, for sure is the end of the world, a place that allows such resentment-- “Welcome to the burning hells~!”
If you want to stop the wailing of the demons here, then follow me.

 sou koko koso yo no hate urami wo yurusu chi youkoso shakunetsu jigoku tomaranu kikoku tometai nara saa tsuitekite

集え集え　怨霊達 この地獄の炎目指して 盛れ盛れ　染まる体 灼熱の赤に負けない

Assemble, assemble, you vengeful ghosts! Aiming for the blazing flames of hell.
Flourish and prosper – your stained bodies won’t lose to the burning heat!

 tsudoe tsudoe onryoutachi kono jigoku no honoo mezashite sakare sakare somaru karada shakunetsu no naka ni makenai

ぬばたまの夜に映える 花火が如く咲き狂え

They bloom like fireworks, illuminating the night

 nubatama no yoru ni haeru hanabi ga gotoku sakikurue

胸潰れ　なお絶えぬ嘆きの魂がひしめく 此処は地獄

Their hearts broken, the unending wails of the souls wander about--this is (the place known as) hell.

 mune tsubure nao taenu nageki no tamashii ga hishimeku koko wa jigoku

そう此処こそ楽園　嘆きを容れる地 ようこそ灼熱地獄 止まらぬ慟哭止めたいなら さあついてきて

So, here is a paradise, a place where lamentation is accepted- “Welcome to the scorching hell!”

If you want to stop the wailings of the grieving souls here, follow me.

 sou koko koso rakuen nageki wo ireru chi youkoso shakunetsu jigoku tomaranu doukoku tometai nara saa tsuitekite

歌え歌え　嗄らした声 振り絞って地の底に響け 踊れ踊れ　痺れた足 耐え忍んで倒れるまで

Sing, sing, strain your voice such that this land shakes with your echoes! Dance, dance! Endure the pain of your already numbed limbs until you fall!

 utae utae karashita koe furishibotte chi no soko ni hibike odore odore shibireta ashi taeshinonde taoreru made

天の静寂に響く 囃子が如く鳴り渡れ

Echo like an orchestra, filling the silence of heaven!

 ten no shijima ni hibiku hayashi ga gotoku nariwatare

走れ走れ　地獄車 駆けぬけてく風よりも疾く 燃えろ燃えろ　火車の焔 灼熱の赤に負けない

Run, run, Hell’s wheel*, dash quickly with the wind!

Burn up, burn up, Hell wheel of blazing flames! Don’t lose to the burning red!

(Rin Kaenbyou, the character who the original song is for, is a hell wheel, a type of youkai/demon. I assume the burning red refers to Hakurei Reimu, the main character from the Touhou series who fights her.)

空から落ちてくる　名も無き花 この手を 伸ばしたら届くのかな

Falling down from the heavens*, these nameless flowers...

My hand extends out to try and catch them--will it make it in time (to catch them?)

*Where heavens=sky=stars, just sounds better.

 sora kara ochitekuru namo naki hana kono te wo nobashitara todoku no ka na

ココロ揺れて涙　いつか消えて咲き誇る 何度も何度も恋して散り往く 振り向く先　あどけない笑顔

My heart sways and cries, one day these flowers will go and blossom in all its glory.

Over and over again I fall in love with the flowers falling in bloom, before I turn away, I smile innocently...

 kokoro yurete namida itsuka kiete sakihokoru nandomo nandomo koishite chiriyuku furimuku saki adokenai egao

後ろ姿遠く　淡く想 (いろ) を纏わせて 可憐に舞う度　ココロが踊った そんな風に季節は巡るよ

A distant, shimmering figure in the background, clad in a palely colored outfit dances sweetly over and over again.

My heart dances in a similar way as the seasons cycle over and over again.

 ushirosugata tooku awaku iro wo matowasete karen ni mau tabi kokoro ga odotta sonna fuu ni kisetsu wa meguru yo

空へと浮かぶのは夢幻 いつかはこの幻想 (けしき) 終わるのかな

Suspended in the sky, these fantasies....

This illusionary landscape I see might come to an end...

 sora e to ukabu no wa yumemaboroshi itsuka wa kono keshiki owaru no ka na

そっと触れてナミダ　側にあると気付かずに 幾度も幾度も巡れど叶わず 夢見た先　儚く愛しき...

Softly feeling tears...I recognize them when they come near...Countless times they come around without coming true, of a dear, fleeting dream that I saw once before...

 sotto furete namida soba ni aru to kizukazu ni ikudo mo ikudo mo meguredo kanawazu yumemita saki hakanaku itoshiki...

未だ想 (こえ) は遠く　この身包み込んでゆく 優しさ愛しさ　全てに恋して 名も無き花　凛と揺れていた

Still, there is a voice in the distance...

This body, becoming enveloped...

Kindness, Beauty...everything I love...

This nameless flower...swaying in a dignified manner.

 imada koe wa tooku kono mi tsutsumikonde yuku yasashisa itoshisa subete ni koishite namo naki hana rinto yureteita

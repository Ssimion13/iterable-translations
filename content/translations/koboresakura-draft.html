今宵桜　舞い落ちた 二度と私に　

向かぬ愛

On this evening, the cherry blossoms

fall in a dance…never again

 ※ koyoi sakura maiochita nidoto watashi ni mukanu ai

未練色した　この涙 言えぬ気持ち　 濡らすだけ

These tears of a unrequited love, colored with regret…
I’m drenched in nothing but unspeakable feelings...

 miren iro shita kono namida ienu kimochi nurasu dake

あなたが残した 大人ぶった簪 捨て なきゃダメよね？ 多分まだ無理だけれど

This hairpin (you used to pretend to be an adult) that you left behind...*
It’d be bad/wrong if I didn’t throw it away, right?

At this point, it’s probably still pointless...

*A kanzashi is a hairpin used by older women and geisha, its that really sharp hairpin.

 anata ga nokoshita otonabutta kanzashi sute na kya dame yo ne? tabun mada muri dakeredo

「じゃあね」　君の声　i黄泉帰る 君を　 忘れられないよ...

“See you later”, your voice revived (as if from the dead)...I could never forget you…

 "jaa ne" kimi no koe yomi kaeru kimi wo wasurerarenai yo...

どうか桜　舞い戻れ 想い涙　溢れてく 出会い別れは　慣れたのに あなただけは　 乾かない

Please, cherry blossoms, come back--

these heavy tears overflow….

Despite being used to your cycle of you coming and going, you have never withered (dryed) (in my eyes)...

 douka sakura maimodore omoi namida afureteku deai wakare wa nareta noni anata dake wa kawakanai

偶然見かけた　幸せそうな二人 お似合い　さよなら なんて言えたらいいのに...

By chance, I caught a glimpse of two happy-looking people…

If only you could say “farewell”

it’d be better...

 guuzen mikaketa shiawase sou na futari oniai sayonara nante ietara ii no ni...

理屈でなだめてくれたって 綺麗事の暴論だよ？

The things you said to pacify me are nothing but pretty nothings, aren’t they?

 rikutsu de nadamete kuretatte kireigoto no bouron da yo?

愛し愛され　終わる愛 伝う嫉妬は　 許さない

Loving, loving back, ending love, and the following jealousy, I can’t allow it.

 aishiaisare owaru ai tsutau shitto wa yurusanai

愛し愛した　孤独劇 波紋のように　 消えていく

Love, Loved, a lonely play. Like ripples, going away...

 aishi aishita kodoku geki hamon no you ni kieteiku

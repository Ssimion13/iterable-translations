Yuuhei Satellite - 空想輪廻
“Kuusou Rinne” – Samsara of Fantasy

Arrange of a character’s song from Imperishable Night
with lyrics from the perspective of a Hakutaku
whose power is the ability to manipulate history.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bai_Ze

定められたような歴史とき　繰り返す

夢　古から　変わるようで変わらぬ

Sadamerareta you na (dare ka no[?]) toki rekishi toki kurikaesu (kurikaesu)

Yume inishie kara (yume no naka) kawaru you de kawaranu

As if they were set in stone

The moments of history (someone’s?)

Repeating over and over (and over again)

From dreams from long ago (within a dream)

that seem to change

but never do

無秩序な力を持つ者

不可欠なその存在にも

Michitsujou na chikara wo motsu mono

Fukaketsu na sono sonzai ni mo

One who possesses the power of disorder (change)

Such an existence is certainly needed as well

次の瞬間に消える 1

君との距離が遠すぎて

嗚呼　忘却の本に書き込んでく

Tsugi no shinkan ni kieru

Kimi to no kyori ga toosugite

Shikkoku no yoru hoshi ga hisomu

Aah boukyaku no hon ni kakikonde yuku

And in the next moment, disappearing

The distance between you and I becomes too far away

On a pitch black night the stars hide themselves

Aah, It’s written in the book of oblivion [being forgotten completely]

定められたような運命　繰り返す

時　今から　変わるようで変わらぬ

Sadamerareta youna (nigete itemo) unmei kurikaesu (kurikaesu)

Toki ima kara (yume kara no __) kawaru you de kawaranu

As if written in stone (even if you run away)

As if written in stone, [fate/destiny],

repeating over and over (and over again)

From the times that seems to change but never do

いつからか失くした力

いつからか見えない心

Itsukara ka nakushita chikara

Itsukara ka mienai kokoro

At some time or another, the power was lost

At some time or another, the heart could no longer be seen

All of existence, shaking unstably

Just close your eyes – the ‘way things are meant to be’ was omitted

the twilight sky(Shunyata/emptiness), shining with light

Aah, and before long tomorrow will come, won’t it?

淡く憐れな人生

無数の文字で描かれた

A human life, so fleeting and ephemeral

written down in an endless number of characters

Raising it with both hands to the silver light

Aah, it is being etched into the Scroll of Samsara

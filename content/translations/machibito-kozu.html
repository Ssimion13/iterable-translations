消え行く影法師

足跡追いかけても見つからず

響いた下駄の音

気持ちは先走れど追いつかず

Kieyuku kageboushi

ashiato oikaketemo mitsukarazu

hibita geta no oto

kimochi ha sakibaredo oitsukazu

A disappearing silhouette…

Even if I chase after its footprints, I’ll never find it…

The sound of the echoing clogs on the ground..

Even if I was honest with my feelings, you’ll never chase them..

今日もまた五月雨

あの人を濡らして

守れない　はがゆさ募る

kyou mata samidare

ano hito wo nurashite

mamorenai haga yusatsunoru

It’s another May rain today….

That person, becoming drenched…

Being unable to protect them, my impatience worsens.

例え二人を繋ぐ関係(もの)が

儚い偶然だとしても

側にいてそっと見つめていたい

つないだ指先はずっと・・・

tatoe futari wo tsunagu mono ga

hakanai guuzen da to shitemo

soba ni ite sotto mitsumetetai

tsunai da yubisaki ha zutto

Even if, for example, the relation that ties us together

was but a fleeting event of chance

I want to be close, intently looking at you

holding hands forever...

夜空の月輪(がちりん)が

照らした一本道行きましょう

笑顔で通りゃんせ

待ち人来ないならば来る日まで

Yozora no Gachirin ga

terashita ihhon michi ikimashou

egao de toryanse

machibito konainagara kuru hi made

The full moon in the night sky shined;

Walking on this straight path

From your smile...if this person I’m waiting for doesn’t come..

then I’ll wait until that day he does...

今日もまた出逢えず

こぼれてく時雨

幾度に巡るや季節

Kyou mo mata deaiezu

koboreteku shigure

ikutabi ni mawaru ya kisetsu

I didn’t see you again today..

(despite) the pouring drizzle

How many times has this happened, how many seasons?

例え二人を繋ぐ関係(もの)が

よくある必然だとしても

Tatoe Futari wo tsunagu mono (kankei) ga

yoku aru hitsuzen da to shitemo

Even if, for example, the relation of the two of us was but one of necessity,

側にいてそっと見つめていたい

つないだ指先はぎゅっと・・・

Soba ni itesotto mitsumetetai

tsunai yubisaki wa gyutto

I want to be close, looking only at you,

our hands tightly held together

待ち人は今いずこへ

Machibito wa Ima izuko he

Where has the one I’ve been waiting for gone?

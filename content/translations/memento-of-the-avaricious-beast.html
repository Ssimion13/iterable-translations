All Touhou songs include commentary on what the creator (ZUN) was thinking whenever he was creating the song, or commentary about the song. These two songs here are

“Memento of an Avaricious Beast”

And

“Memento For All Living Things ~ Memory of Fossil Energy.”
The songs are used as the boss music / theme song of a character named “Toutetsu Yuuma” (meaning. “Taotie, Consummate Demon”).

Just look at the character in the background. Tell me what colors you see, what she’s holding, just take in that outfit and tell me, with this description below…

Ever since 40 years ago, we’ve been threatened/frightened that petroleum will be depleted in 30 years. Do you think that was a lie or no?

Discovering new petroleum in the ground, birthing new technological methods to extract it,

If you look at the whole world, our current fuel consumption is increasing.

「有機体全てのメメント　～ Memory of Fossil Energy」

Memento for all Organic Organisms1 ~ Memory of Fossil Energy

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DNUCkNnKExM

この世は不思議に満ちています。その中でも最大の謎の一つが生命でしょう。

Kono yo wa fushigi ni michite imasu. Sono naka demo saidai no nazo no hitotsu ga seimei deshou.

This world is filled with mysteries. But within those, the greatest puzzle is that of ‘life’.

生命とは何か。生物と無生物にはどういう差があるのか。

Seimei to wa nanika. Seibutsu to museibutsu ni wa douiu sa ga aru no ka.

What is ‘life’? What is the difference between a living thing and a nonliving thing?

誰しもが一度は考え、そして科学的か、哲学的か、宗教的に落とし所を見つけていることでしょう。

Dareshi mo ga ichido wa kangae, soshite kagakuteki ka, tetsugakuteki ka, shuukyouteki ni otoshiba wo mitsukete iru koto deshou.

Everyone thinks about it at one time, whether scientifically, philosophically, religiously, finding a point of compromise between the three.

その中の古い説として、生物は有機物（炭素化合物）で構成されている有機体と言うのがあります。石油は太古の生物が長い時間を掛けて変化したものだと言われています。

Sono naka no furui setsu toshite, seibutsu wa yuukibutsu (tansoka goubutsu) de kousei sarete iru yuugitai to iu no ga arimasu. Sekiyu wa taiko no seibutsu ga nagai jikan wo kakete henka shita mono dato iwareteimasu.

In an old thesis, living things are composed of bodies made of ‘organic matter’ (carbon-based compounds). Petroleum is what they call living things that have changed forms over a very long period of time.

だとすれば油田は、まさに血の池さながらの有機物の海と言うことになります。同様に石炭も植物由来の有機物です。

Da to sureba yuden wa, masa ni chi no ike sanagara no yuukibutsu no umi to iu koto ni narimasu. Douyou ni ishizumi shokubutsu yurai no yuukibutsu desu.

If we take that, then oil fields become something that could be called, like the pond of blood2, a ‘sea of organic matter’ . In the same way, coal is organic matter derived from plant matter.

人間は生物であった有機物を燃やして生きているのです。血の池地獄とはまさに現代社会......

Ningen wa seibutsu de atta yuukibutsu wo moyashite ikiteiru no desu. Chi no ike jigoku to wa masa ni genzaishakai…..

Humans live by burning the organic matter of living things. The pond of blood is just like modern society….

このまま炭素依存していたら灼熱の星になってしまうのでしょうか。いやはや恐ろしい。

Sono mama tanso ison shite itara shakunetsu no hoshi ni natte shimau no deshou ka. Iyahaya osoroshii.

At this rate, if we become carbon-dependent, this will become a burning hot planet… It’s incredibly terrifying.

そんな中、面白いニュースが入ってきました。「北極海でガソリンと同じ成分作る植物プランクトン発見（＊２０２１年７月）とのこと。さてさて、強欲な者達はこのニュースをどう受け止め、技術を進化させるのでしょうか。

Sonna naka, omoshiroi NYUUSU ga haitte kimashita. (hokkokukai de GASORIN to onaji seibun tsukuru shokubutsu PURANKUTON hakken (*7/2021) to no koto. Satesate, gouyoku na monotachi wa kono NYUUSU wo dou uketome, gijutsu wo shinka saseru no deshou ka.

In the middle of all that, I heard some interesting news. [Phytoplankton that create something identical to petroleum were found in the arctic ocean] (as of 7/2021). Now then, how will those greedy people3 take this news, will they let technology evolve?

ZUN（７歳の息子に、水素燃料だから聖火は消えないんだよ、と教えて貰いました。そうなのか）

ZUN (7 sai no musuko ni, suisou nenryou dakara seika wa kienain dayo, to oshietemoraimashita. Sou nano ka)

ZUN (my 7 year old taught me that the Olympic torch doesn’t go out because it uses hydrogen. Is that sooo4?)

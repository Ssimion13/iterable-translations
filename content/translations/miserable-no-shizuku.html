ミゼラブルの雫

“Miserable Droplets, or Droplets of Misery…?”

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2yDW7gBuzE

君はなぜ泣いているの？ 知らんぷり　もう出来ない いたずらに笑い合える　君がいい

Why are you crying?

I can’t pretend like I don’t see it….

I liked(?) the you that was always messing around, trying to make everyone laugh.

---------

 kimi wa naze naite iru no? shiranpuri mou dekinai itazura ni waraiaeru kimi ga ii

『同情』　や　『共感』　は後付け とにかく君が心配

“Sympathy” and “Empathy” are afterthoughts*; in any case, I’m worried about you.

(*atozuke means “appendix” like the back of a book..?)

--------

 "doujou" ya "kyoukan" wa atozuke tonikaku kimi ga shinpai

素直に　「笑って」　と言えば 元通りになると思ってたよ...

I thought that if I honestly told you “smile!” everything would turn back to normal...

--------

 sunao ni "waratte" to ieba motodoori ni naru to omotteta yo...

哀しみが零れ落ちて　足元を濡らしてゆく 幼気　（いたいけ）　で深い　君の 見過ごせぬ小さな海は 安っぽい慰めでは　汲み取れはしないと知り 励ましや　 問うことさえ 躊躇ためらった

Your sorrow overflowed, soaking the ground at your feet. When I saw the unmissable small sea that the innocent you(?), had created, I realized that no insignificant consolation or sympathy I could create could help me understand.

I couldn’t even encourage you or ask a question, so I hesitated.

 kanashimi ga koboreochite ashimoto wo nurashite yuku itaike de fukai kimi no misugosenu chiisana umi wa yasuppoi nagusame de wa kumitore wa shinai to shiri hagemashiya tou koto sae tameratta

優しく接してあげたい だけれど　どうすればいい？

I want to gently hold you...but in what way would it be alright?

 yasashiku sesshite agetai dakeredo dou sureba ii?

迷った言葉なんかより 優しく頭を撫でてあげた

Instead of using things like uncertain words, I gently brushed your head…(?)

 mayotta kotoba nanka yori yasashiku atama wo nadete ageta

哀しみが零れ落ちて　足元を濡らしてゆく 幼気で深い　君の見過ごせぬ小さな海は 寄り添って弱い気持ち　汲み取ってゆけばいいさ 『手を握る』　それだけでも 今はいい...

Your sorrow overflowed, soaking the ground at your feet.

When I saw the small sea that you, so helpless, had made, I held your weak feelings. Just “holding your hand” for now, is fine.

 kanashimi ga koboreochite ashimoto wo nurashite yuku itaikeki de fukai kimi no misugosenu chiisana umi wa yorisotte yowai kimochi kumitotte yukeba ii sa "te wo nigiru" soredake demo ima wa ii...

時は経ち　次第に君は心を解いてくれた 時間には敵わないね　優秀だ

As time passes, you gradually opened your heart to me. There’s no way I can win against the passage of time, is there?

Its just better than I am.

 toki wa tachi shidaini kimi wa kokoro wo toitekureta jikan ni wa kanawanai ne yuushuu da

ちっぽけで弱い自分 無力さを知ってしまった でも君は　「ありがとう」　って 微笑んで　涙ぬぐった

I felt what it meant to be weak, frail, and helpless...but when you said “thank you” to me, I smiled, wiping my tears.

 chippoke de yowai jibun muryoku sa wo shitte shimatta demo kimi wa "arigatou" tte hohoen de namida nugutta
「ねえ、いつもの冗談で笑わせて？」 ほらやっぱり　無邪気にからかう君は素敵だよ "nee, itsumo no joudan de warawasete?" hora yappari mujaki ni karakau kimi wa suteki da yo

“Say, why don’t you say that joke that you always do?”

I knew it; the joking, innocent you is the you I like the most.

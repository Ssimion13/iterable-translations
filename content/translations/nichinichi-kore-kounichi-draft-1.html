『日日是好日』

https://youtu.be/BXKnMl-6Rc0

(This translates literally to “day” “day” “here” “good/like” “day” in chinese.)
(The japanese read it as “nichi nichi kore kou nichi”)

(It roughly translates to “Everyday is a good day” or “Enjoy your day”)

“Everyday is a good day” is a phrase out there.

Every day throughout our lives,

We normally we we try to make better

Being able to take [“Right now, the reality that

At this moment I am alive]

No matter what, we are [today more than yesterday,

Now more than last time], being able to have a method of

Capturing that

Now and today and the past, seeming to want to compare them

But above that, if it is good/works, [good day] isn’t something that

Can be called that

For example, even if it becomes a worse day than yesterday

Even if the situation has become worse than before

That is a [good day].

I think that [The moment we call ‘now’] is [the reality we call ‘now’] is

[a good time] and recognizing this is very important/precious/valuable.

This time, this moment, could be called living life to the fullest.

The acting/conducting yourself that you could call Living life to the fullest

Is a thing that you could say makes the future and makes tomorrow.

Like that, living life endeavoring to do this is what I feel the phrase that says

“Every day is a good day” is expressing.

In the morning, we wake up

At night, we go to sleep

It is for this reason, you know?
It is because we have this time

I believe we could say “it’s a good day”, can’t we?

Nihil Kagura: Yuuhei Satellite

( ニヒル神楽)

Its a strange compound word. Nihil is latin for nothing, and a Kagura is a festival put on for the Shinto Gods, from my understanding (literally means “God-Entertainment.)
Put them together, with the context of the song lyrics, the nihil kagura is a meaningless dance put on for the entertainment of the Gods.

Or something. Countless things lost in translation, not even mentioning how bad I am at translating.

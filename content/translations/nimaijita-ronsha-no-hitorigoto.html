Soliloquy of a Two-Face Advocate

虚無と引き換えに

信念の無い自分を弄ぶんだ

As an exchange for nothingness

I toyed with my faithless, convictionless self

悪意無き嘘で

塗り潰したセカイは滑稽で愛おしい

This world, painted with lies bearing no ill intent

Is comedically lovely

品の無い根っこを

隠して心の隙間を探すのさ

I’d say I’m searching for the gaps

In a heart that hides it’s unrefined foundation/ (plant root)

You know, the two sided tongue –

is just messing around arbitrarily

Song Explanation / Extra Context:

This is an arrange of the final boss Zanmu’s theme from Touhou 19 – the original song name is

逸脱者達の無礙光 ～ Kingdam of Nothingness
(The light that frees one of obstruction of those who deviate ~ Kingdom of Nothingness)

Zanmu is an oni who ‘rules’ over hell. Well spoken, fiercely independent, and in control over every situation. Her ‘power’ is the ability to manipulate 無 (the concept of ‘nothingness’).

She ends up ‘winning’ the incident at the end of the day, but changes her mind on her plan to turn Gensokyo into a sort of ‘safe cage’ because of the work of two characters:
1.) Reimu (the main character), being someone so immersed in fantasy and in living wholly from moment to moment

And

2.) The other oni Ibuki Suika who is similarly godlike in powers, but doesn’t get why Zanmu even cares about the incident in the first place and basically just appears to ‘have fun’.

Line by line analysis:

In exchange for nihility

I toy with myself, faithless as I am

Have you ever felt that ‘moment’ where you truly ‘zeroed’ yourself out fully? To remove ones ego from decision making to the best of one’s own abilities?

If nothing matters, then I can have fun with myself, right? Anything is permissible since everything returns to zero. Even my concept of self returns to zero. There’s no reason to be a dick any more than there is a reason to be one. I am in ‘control’ of such a small, miniscule section of reality with these two ape paws and two ape legs and everything about myself is arbitrary and I can change who I view as ‘myself’ at any time if I truly felt like it (go to a place where no one knows your naaaaameee), because there’s no reason not to do it if I feel like it.

This world, painted with maliceless lies

Is comically lovely

The whole world is built upon lie after lie that are told not just by society but by people we talk to every day. Some are intentional, the things we tell ourselves that we know aren’t true but we say anyways – these sorts of things, rationalizations, and often the fundamental ideas and stories we rally behind as people when we try to point at something that we can say we ‘belong’ to.

I’d say the heart, hiding it’s unrefined foundation
Searches for the gaps within itself

People search for things to plug the holes in their heart. But if someone actually found what they were looking for, would they even be happy with it? If the foundational mindset is bad, then would things really improve ‘if only X Y Z’?

We know our mental processes deep down are inconsistent and that we don’t live up to our own idealism if we even have anything to be idealistic over…

二枚の舌がさ

勝手にジャレ合うのだから無実でしょ？

You know, the two sided tongue is just playing on it’s on accord

It’s insubstantial (guiltless), isn’t it?

I might not always say what I mean, especially when I am talking to someone and I have to keep up with the pace of conversation, but the ‘autopilot words’ and phrasing I’m using when I’m talking that might not be ‘truly what I think’ are not me trying to be rude, it’s just something ‘I said in the moment under pressure’. I can’t blame someone for ‘saying the right words’ in certain situations even if it isn’t true because that’s how a lot of life is. There’s a difference between a lie and a white lie.

Part of being ‘real’ is being fake, too. Sometimes, the truth is not what is important but what is expressed in the words we say.

And sometimes, you just want to say what people want to hear because there’s no point in being honest with someone who isn’t honest with themselves, no?

この空の下で体裁ばかりを磨く

おぞましい生き方よりはマシだろ？

Under this sky [emptiness],

It’s better than living the repulsive life

Doing nothing polishing outward appearances
Right?

When faced with the endless cycle of Samsara and the fact that nothing in this world is permanent, why care about appearance, status, or wealth for it’s own sake? Why not take what you want to take and find your own enjoyment?

あの雲の下で水掛け論者が嘆く

気張るなよ

Under those clouds

Those who argue fruitlessly lament

Don’t wear yourself out

What’s the point of arguing? What is there to gain in arguments for their own sake?
If an argument is winnable or has actionable benefits, then sure, but why fight if there isn’t one?

What is the point?

気ままに見下ろす方が気持ちいいから

残念だね

Because it feels better to condescend and live as you like

It’s a shame, isn’t it?

What makes Zanmu so compelling as a character is that by controlling mu, she fundamentally at the very least is either a Bodhisattva or alternatively someone who has learned the ‘secret buddhist arts’ and just decided ‘yeah I’m just gonna take all of these for myself, TYVM’.
I’m pretty familiar at this point with the connotations of this, so I find this line to have a particular weight to it; if you know something that other people don’t know and there’s no point in explaining why you think your mindset or knowledge is important or superior, what gain is it to share it with others only to watch them not take you seriously?

There is satisfaction in ‘knowing what other people don’t’. There is satisfaction in seeing the world through a more unfiltered lens, and satisfaction in looking down on people who just can’t see the forest for the trees.

If the world is truly just bound by silly rules hiding a ‘dog eat dog’ mentality under the hood, then the winning move is certainly to ‘take what you want and not give a shit about other people’, right?

….Is that actually right?

I feel it’s important to note that Zanmu is proven wrong by the end of the story and (in classic touhou form) basically decides to singlehandedly end the incident they started themselves. How is she proven wrong?

Imagine you’re an oni, one of the most feared and powerful beings in existence able to functionally control the concept of ‘not’. You have the world and all the events going on in your pocket and you’re about to close the noose on your goals…

….and another oni shows up, takes a look at you, and with a confused and slightly condescending tone goes: “Dude why are you trying so hard? Take a drink and party with us.”
It’s one thing when a mortal tells you this, it’s another when your demigod peer who knows and can do roughly the same things you can tells you this.

This goes to one of my oldest questions I’ve had since really taking any time to learn about Buddhist thinking, which is “Why do Buddhists not bother evangelizing?” Why do Hindu’s (for the most part) not evangelize, either?

It’s because if you really knew how the world worked, who would believe you? Who would even take the time to listen to you unless you started throwing down miracles? Your voice is just one opinion in a herd of opinions.

We are only on this world for such a limited amount of time, why waste time trying to convince other people of the ‘truth’ that they don’t even want to hear? You’re not gonna ‘save’ someone (as there is nothing to ‘save’ and no ‘someone’), and isn’t the idea of trying to force a situation like this an attachment in and of itself?

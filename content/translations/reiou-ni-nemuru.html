蝶よ鳥よ　恋一夜（こいひとよ）

揺蕩う時の随に

ひらりはらり　空を舞う

乱れ咲いて　宵桜

chou yo tori yo koihitoyo

tayutau toki no ma ni ma ni

hirari harari sora wo mau

midarezaite yoizakura

This evening, the Butterflies and the birds, flutter about at the mercy of time

Lightly, Gently, dancing in the sky

Blooming erratically, these evening blossoms...

醒めて消える　愚かな夢でも

春が終わる前に見させてね

指の隙間からすり抜けてく

時間の砂　拾うすべもなく

samete kieru oroka na yume demo

haru ga owaru mae ni misasete ne

yubi no sukima kara surinuketeku

jikan no suna hirou sube mo naku

Even if these are stupid dreams that come and go,

Show me them before spring ends, okay?

The sands of time slipping through the gaps in my fingers,

without a way to collect them...

光の蝶　鱗粉を零し

羽休めの花を求めてる

深い夜に　足を踏み込めば

心の森　もう別天地

hikari no chou rinpun wo koboshi

hane yasume no hana wo motometeru

fukai yoru ni ashi wo fumikomeba

kokoro no mori mou bettenchi

The scales of the butterfly being shed-

If I intruded in the heart of the forest In the deepest time of night,

searching for the plumes of the missing flowers,

I’d already be in a different world...

生者必滅　会者定離

その理に縛られて

虚しい現実（リアル）に溺れるのは　もう

やめにしようか

shoujahitsumetsu eshajouri

sono kotowari ni shibararete

munashii riaru ni oboreru no wa mou

yame ni shiyou ka

All things that live will die; all things that meet will part

That truth binds everything together.

Drowning in the reality of what life is, why not give up already?

白玉の楼に

辿り着いて　憂き世離れ

見渡す大地

悲しみに溢れていても

いつか季節が

また花を咲かせるのでしょう

その時までは

華胥の国に眠りたい

hakugyoku no rou ni

tadoritsuite ukiyo banare

miwatasu daichi

kanashimi ni afureteitemo

itsuka kisetsu ga

mata hana wo sakaseru no deshou

sono toki made wa

kasho no kuni ni nemuritai

At Hakugyoku no rou (Tower of White Gems, kinda of like the land of the dead)

I’m still struggling to accept my separation from my transient life

Looking over the Earth,

even though all I feel is sorrow for my loss when I look

When will the season come where the flowers will bloom again?

Until that day, I want to sleep in this land of perfection

midarezaite yoizakura

This evening, the Butterflies and the birds, flutter about at the mercy of time

Lightly, Gently, dancing in the sky

Blooming erratically, these evening blossoms...

胸に沁みる　誰かの歌声（うたごえ）

春の時雨　睫毛を濡らした

去りし日々の仮初の記憶

蔦のように絡み付いてくる

mune ni shimiru dareka no utagoe

haru no shigure matsuge wo nurashita

sarishi hibi no karisome no kioku

tsuta no you ni karamitsuitekuru

Someone’s voice pierces my heart

Spring’s rain, drenching my eyelashes

The temporary memory of days lost

Twirl and coil around me like ivy

切り裂くように　鳥は空を往く

あの情景　また蘇るの

人の夢を　「儚い」　と読めば

この心は　楽になる？

kirisaku you ni tori wa sora wo yuku

ano joukei mata yomigaeru no

hito no yume wo "hakanai" to yomeba

kono kokoro wa raku ni naru?

And the birds disappear into the sky as if they were torn to pieces;

That scene--If I was to read “Transient” from the dream of the person who was resurrected,

would this heart of mine find comfort?

幽明境を異にして

その魂は何処へ行く

この世に生きたことを忘れて　また

生まれ変わるの

yuumei sakai wo koto ni shite

sono tamashii wa doko e yuku

konoyo ni ikita koto wo wasurete mata

umarekawaru no

Making a distinction between the worlds of life and death

Where is that soul to go?

Already forgotten its life in this world,

will it have a new beginning?

それでも　それでも

叶わない夢でもいいの

soredemo soredemo

kanawanai yume demo ii no

But even so, but even so

It’s best that this dream doesn’t come true

咲かない西行妖（さくら）の

下で眠り続けたい

sakanai sakura no

shita de nemuritsuzuketai

Wanting to remain at rest, beneath the unblossoming Saigyou Ayakashi (Cherry Blossom, referring to the one from TH07),

春、夏、秋、冬

季節さえ忘れるくらい

此岸の声も

もう聞こえなくなるくらい

眠って　眠って

夢が現実になるまで

いつかは見える

何よりも美しい君（はな）…

haru, natsu, aki, fuyu

kisetsu sae wasureru kurai

shigan no koe mo

mou kikoenakunaru kurai

nemutte nemutte

yume ga genjitsu ni naru made

itsuka wa mieru

nani yori mo utsukushii hana…

Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter

To the point that I’m forgetting all the seasons

Until I won’t be able to hear the voice of this world any longer..

Sleeping… Sleeping,

Until this dream becomes reality

When I can see it--

you, more beautiful than anything else.

そよぐ風に　花吹雪

純粋たる　その願い

死生の輪廻　繰り返す

瞼の裏　幻桜（ゆめざくら）

soyobu kaze ni hana fubuki

junsui taru sono negai

shishou no rinne kurikaesu

mabuta no ura yumezakura

The rain of cherry blossoms in the rustling wind are

a true granting of my wish

The cycle of life and death will repeat,

Behind my eyelids, lies the sakura of my dreams (an illusion)...

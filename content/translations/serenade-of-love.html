昨日見た夢の話　最後まで聞かせてよ 喜びも　悲しみさえも　願いにこめて

Yesterday, I saw a story in my dream; I watched/listened to it until the very end. Delightful and even sorrowful wishes came together in it.

 kinou mita yume no hanashi saigo made kikasete yo yorokobi mo kanashimi sae mo negai ni komete

月明かり浮かんだ　もし君ともう一度
出会えるなら 星に願いを　そっと囁けば 雨も涙も　雪へと変わり舞い上がる

The moonlight would float in the air if you and I were to meet once again if I softly murmur a prayer to the stars in the sky. The rain and my tears change to snow and dance in the sky...

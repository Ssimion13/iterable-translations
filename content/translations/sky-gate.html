Sky Gate by FELT

心に写した　思いをそっと伝えて

隠された扉を叩く

Kokoro ni utsushita, omoi wo sotto tsutaete

kakusareta tobira wo tataku

The feelings photographed in my heart softly knock on a hidden door...

まだ暗い空に　光を見つけ

明けていく世界　未来に起こされ

頬を抜けてくほら　春風に涙溶かして

進んでく道を今　照らして

mada kurai sora ni hikari wo mitsuke

ageteiku sekai mirai ni okosare

hoho wo nugeteku hora harukazeni namida tokashite

susundeku michi wo ima terashite

Still searching for light in the darkened sky,

Awakening a future in a empty world...

Looking as my cheeks lose their color, my tears, dissolved into the spring wind

Illuminating the path I walk on today

駆け抜ける風　思いを伝えて

忘れられた世界で

過去　未来　真実　見つめている

涙で濡れた　風を感じて

笑顔のその嘘も

すべて今抱きしめ伝えて

kakenukeru kaze omoi wo tsutatete

wasurereta sekai de

kako mirai shinjitsu mitsumete iru (yo)

namida de nureta kaze wo kanjite

egao no sono usomo

subete ima dakishime tsutaete

The wind passing through me conveys your feelings,

in a forgotten world…

The Past, The Future, The Truth, intently watching all of it…

Feeling the wind, wettened with tears

Even with that lying smiling face of yours,

you’re always embracing me dearly...

広がる世界　悲しみ背負った背中に

写した揺るぎない心

遠く空をかけて　風を追って

切り取られる今が　未来へと繋ぐ

走りだした声　空を翔る風追い越して

地平線を横目に　広がる

An expanding world, burdened with the sorrow I carry on my back,

captured a picture of my unwavering heart

Running after a far away sky,

Chasing after a wind that blows far away,

connecting the severed present to the future,

There is a voice that ran by in the sky, outrunning the wind,

glancing the horizon, expanding….

問いかけているよ　答えを求めて

涙混じる嘘も

今信じる思いだけをあげるよ

この世界から　私が消えても

もし許されるなら

君の生きる未来を信じて

I’m asking you a question, I’m searching for an answer

Even my lies are mixed in with tears...

Right now, I can only give you these feelings I believe in

Even if I were to disappear, if you would allow me to,

I would believe in a future where you live on (instead).

通り雨に打たれ　ただ立ちすくんで

明日が怖くなったの

一人じゃ進めないよ

一筋の光と　笑いかける空に

進んでく今遠くへ　光の先へ

Beaten down from the showering rain, simply standing, petrified

The tomorrow before me has become terrifying,

I can’t go on alone…

Suddenly, in a beam of light and a smiling sky,

I find myself going forth, far away, to the light I saw once before...

駆け抜ける風　思いを伝えて

嘘まみれの世界で

過去　未来　真実　見つめているよ

The wind, flowing past me, conveying your feelings

in the world stained with lies

The past, the future, the truth/reality, looking intently at it all

涙で濡れた　風を感じて

笑顔のその嘘も

すべて今抱きしめ伝えて

Feeling the wind that is drenched in tears,

That lying smiling face of yours,

Always feeling you, holding me close...

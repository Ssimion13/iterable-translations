※２ 優しくされてる　深読みしてゆく 予感に溺れて　胸を焦がしてく... 確かめるように　指先重ねる ひとつになれない　隙間を埋めてく

The kindness you’ve given me, I’m reading too much into it...I am drowning in the hunches I get...I yearn for them [to be true]...

In order to confirm them, I stack my fingertips on yours to see what happens....but it doesn’t fill the gap between us.

I hate lies, just show me what the truth is. I feel an uneasy feeling that is more than just appearance--I’m delicate.

※３ 疑うことは　出来るわけない 盲目の恋　まだ冷めないで 自分飾って　弱さ隠して 暴いて欲しい　言えないけれど

It is so easy to find reasons to doubt blind love, but its something that can’t be suppressed. I try to hide my weakness; but deep down I wish i could tell you how I feel.

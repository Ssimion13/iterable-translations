Context:
This is an arrange of the song Tiny Shangri-La, the character theme of the character “Son Biten” in Touhou 19.

Touhou 19 UDoALG OST - Son Biten's Theme - Tiny Shangri-La

The wiki page for the character covers a rough idea of what the character’s backstory is (as a character) but because the character is based on one of the most timeless and important literary characters in all human history (Son Wukong, the Monkey King), it is important to really note some of the details as well.

Son Wukong is one of the protagonists of the tale “A Journey to the West”.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journey_to_the_West

While the majority of the story focuses in on the journey of the pilgrim Xuanzang and his quest to acquire Buddhist sutras from India (the west) while accompanied by several anthropomorphic animals (who all allegorically represent different parts of human thinking), the first eight chapters are exclusively focused on the rise and fall of The Monkey King, Sun Wukong (Son Goku 11in Japanese)

As this is important (and plainly obvious to Asian readers), the characters that make up Son Wukong are:

孫 – (Son) Grandchild / Descendent

悟 - (Go) Awareness, Enlightenment, Realization

空 - (Ku[u]): Void, nothingness, meaningless, in particular Shunyata (the Buddhist concept that nothing is immutable)

Note:

You can find the chapters here:
https://chine.in/fichiers/jourwest.pdf

But because it is a long story I don’t expect anyone to read it, so I’ll give a quick summary of the tale.

Wukong was born from a stone egg given divine power by heaven by random chance. Wukong grew up with the other monkeys living happily until one day he found a secret entrance to heaven behind a waterfall; finding this, he grabbed his monkey buddies and set up shop in some abandoned house, and essentially created a little Shangri-La (paradise) for his friends. He becomes their king by consent and they live a blessed life….but then they realize that they will all one day die, and this inevitability eats away at Wukong until he is advised that there are Taoist sages who have found a way to gain immortality.

He goes on a journey to find a teacher, and spends much time training with one until he learns the secrets of his teacher and not only gains immortality but the ability to cast insane reality bending magic. Kicked out by his teacher after learning this, he returns home with his secrets. He defeats a monster who took over the monkey kingdom in his absence, but finding no weapon to be worthy of him, he goes to the den of what is functionally Chinese Mythological Bureaucracy Poseidon12 and extorts him for both godly weapon and armor on threat of violence. He gets his namesake staff which can change size at will here.

This pisses off Chinese Mythological Bureaucracy Poseidon, who complains to the Jade Emperor (think Zeus) – they send Grim Reapers after Wukong (as he is immortal and ‘cheating death’) but he not only manages to defeat them, but manages to sneak into heaven and erase his name and all his monkey friends from the book of life and death. Pissing off heaven even more, they send an army to defeat him but fail; the Jade Emperor decides to invite him to heaven and make him a God…..of the stable of heaven.

Most people would be satisfied with reaching godhood at all, but Sun Wukong is unhappy and decides to declare himself “Great Sage, Equal of Heaven”. To appease him, the Jade Emperor tells him he has a new important job as “Guardian of the Heavenly Peach Garden” (the peaches grant eternal life); he goes up there and is happy until he realizes there is a banquet and he wasn’t invited (and he is seen as a joke immortal pretending to be a god), so he proceeds to eat all the peaches, sneak into the banquet before it starts, eat a ton of food, sneak into the top levels of heaven, and steal yet even more ways to become immortal, and runs away back to the surface world to prepare for rebellion against heaven.

The Jade Emperor is pissed and sends troops to deal with him. After beating the crap out of heaven’s forces, one hero manages to defeat him, and capturing him, they put him in a cauldron of pure fire and try to distill him down so they can get them back after he stole immortality stuff from heaven. After 49 days they open the cauldron and not only is he even more invincible but has more powers too.

(Just copy pasting from wikipedia now, but this is better than what I can do)

The Jade Emperor and the authorities of Heaven appeal to the Buddha, who arrives from his temple in the West in person. After listening to Sun Wukong, who makes a case that he should be the new Jade Emperor, the Buddha makes a bet that the Monkey King cannot escape from his palm. The Monkey King smugly accepts the bet. He leaps and flies all the way to the edge of the universe. Seeing nothing there but five towering pillars, the Monkey King believes that he has reached the end of all existence. To prove his trail, he marks a pillar with a phrase declaring himself the Great Sage Equal to Heaven and urinates on the middle pillar. He then leaps back and returns to Buddha's palm to claim his victory in winning the bet. Sun Wukong is then very surprised to find that the five "pillars" he found are merely fingers of the Buddha's hand, finding it impossible to believe. When the Monkey King tries to escape the palm, Buddha turns his hand into a mountain of rocks, sending Sun Wukong hurtling back down to earth. Before the Monkey King can lift the mountain off, the Buddha seals him there, using a paper talisman bearing the mantra, Om Mani Padme Hum, in gold letters. The Monkey King remains imprisoned in stocks for five hundred years to 'learn patience and humility with only his head and hands protruding from the base of the mountain. The Buddha arranges two earth spirits to feed the Monkey King iron pellets when he is hungry, and molten copper when he is thirsty.

He is set free by Xuanzang on his journey to the west on accident, and accompanies him on the journey, serving as the ‘muscle’ of the party and as an impulsive and violent foil to the peaceful monk.

Now, with that said, I can talk about who Son Biten is in Touhou 19, as understanding and knowing the story of Son Wukong is just…. Taken as a given as far as the game is concerned. I had to look it up to understand the relevance of it.

….And I have to explain the plot of Touhou 19.

Touhou 19’s plot…in as few words as possible. Here goes.

After Touhou 18’s story causes the land of fantasy Gensokyo to ‘lose the concept of land possession’ due to the powers of the final boss of the game, the 3 yakuza-esque animal spirit clans of Touhou 17 who believe solely in the rule of the law of the jungle seize the opportunity to invade Gensokyo, and the shrine maiden protecting Gensokyo, Reimu Hakurei goes out to investigate and figure out how to stop them.

Touhou 19 has 19 characters, each with their own story. Reimu’s story is first, and it is simple: she goes around and beats up people, and, through the power of her ability to manipulate fantasy13, defies all logic, reasoning, or deduction and just totally chances on finding the actual mastermind of the incident, Zanmu Nippaku.
(https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Zanmu_Nippaku)

Zanmu is the king of hell, and according to her, hell is a heaven for those who deviate.
“Beating” Zanmu, Reimu demands that Zanmu stop the invasion as the mastermind, to which Zanmu says not to worry about the invasion and to leave the matter up to her, as “they are already within the palm of my hand.”

Zanmu’s power is the ability to manipulate 無, or “nothingness/emptiness/shunyata”. Whether or not she’s actually doing this or is just a really good mastermind is irrelevant, as the rest of the story and all of the other character arcs are, for the most part, about all the beast themed characters of Touhou looking to make a profit off of the war, with the characters aligned with the yakuza clans aiming to defeat each other, only to fail and fall into Zanmu’s trap (explained below)

Each one of the clans represents a way to get ahead in a ‘world that is dog eat dog’. One clan values only physical/violent strength, one views greed as the secret to success….and one clan in particular believes that being underhanded/sneaky and exploiting the system is the secret to success.

The leader of this clan finds a monkey that has eaten spirits that they are forbidden to eat, becoming sentient but also damning themselves to hell. This leader tells the monkey that they are a descendent of Sun Wukong named “Son Biten”, and gives them all the tools/etc.that Sun Wukong had and promising to save them from falling to hell. They then pronounce that they will win because they have the Monkey King at their side.

Fully embracing this persona and fully enjoying sentience and freedom, Son Biten proceeds to beat up basically everyone they fight against, but over time they realize that they’ve been used by Zanmu14 by being given a necklace that sends people to hell when they touch it.15 Realizing the futility of the conflict , Son Biten proceeds to have fun with it and just embrace fighting and being alive instead of doing her boss’s dirty work. Her boss ends the story as a shadow of their former self, forced to realize that all their hard work and planning to conquer the surface has fallen apart and will never be realized16, and Son Biten sticks around to pick up her spirits even though she knows that her boss cannot save her from her fate.

It is hard to really explain well, but Yachie, Son Biten’s boss and leader of the sneaky/exploit the system clan, is herself a Tortoise Dragon youkai, which in eastern symbolism represents the divine . And since the divine is totally bureaucratic and totally ‘by the books’, there is such a huge sense of irony/stupidity in Yachie choosing to bring the very embodiment of rebellion against the system and expecting that character to actually be willing to go along with her for longer than 5 minutes.

Yeah that’s all I got for now, that should give context to what the song is about at least.

季は巡り　花開く

想い巡り　花は咲く

ki wa meguri hanahiraku

omoi meguri hana wa saku

The seasons cycle

The flowers open

The feelings cycle

The flowers bloom

-----------------

夢に遊ぶ蝶の翅

無垢の彩麗しくも

yume ni asobu chou no hane

muku no iro uruwashiimo

Playing in a dream

on the wings of a butterfly

Their innocent/pure* coloring

so beautiful

*Muku means “innocent/pure” but also means a type of kimono

----------------

さだめの糸絡むまま

果てた常世の徒花

sadame no ito karamu mama

hateta tokoyo no adabana

Entwined in the threads of fate

The forever unchanging end

of a fruitless flower

------------------

白い空に舞う淡雪のように

手のひらの上　儚く消えた肖像

薄紅に濡れた花景色の奥

末期看取る瞳に映した願い

Shiroi sora ni mau awayuki no youni

tenohira no ue hakanaku kiete shouzou

usube ni nureta hana keshiki no oku

matsugo mitoru hitomi ni utsushita negai

In my palm lies a fleeting, disappearing figure

Dancing like the snow in a wintry sky

In the details of a scene of a flower

wettened with crimson

Her eyes that await her final moments

Reflected a wish

------------------

哀しき命よ　どうか安らかに眠れ

現の罪は忘我の果てに置き去り

愛しき命よ　そうか安らかに眠れ

凍えた胸に春風の満ちる日まで

kanashiki inochi yo douka yasaraka ni nemure

utsutsu no tsumi wa bouga no hate ni okizari

itoshiki inochiyo souka yasaraka ni nemure

kogoeta mune ni harukaze no michiru hi made

Oh, pitiable/miserable life,

please, somehow, rest in peace...

Abandon your worldly sins

in the ecstasy of the end...

Oh, dearest life,

rest in peace any way you can

until the spring wind of a full sun

reaches your frozen heart…

------------------

幾世の時を経てまた訪れる

爛漫の桜の園で

響く歌はうたかたの夢の最果てであなたに

きっと逢えること願って

Ikuyo no toki wo hete mata otozureru

Ranman no sakura no sono de

Hibiku uta wa utakata no yume no hatete de anata ni

Kitto aeru koto negatte

Generations of time pass,

only to come again

To a garden of cherry trees

again in resplendent bloom

I pray that this echoing song

will certainly meet you,

who lies in the furthest ends

of an ephemeral dream
